--- 1. TradingAgents review (TauricResearch/TradingAgents) — DONE 2026-06-26 ---
Goal: study how TradingAgents is designed, how our model differs, and what to
adopt.

TradingAgents design: LLM analyst team (Fundamentals/Sentiment/News/Technical)
-> Bull vs Bear researcher DEBATE (N rounds) -> Trader -> LLM risk team ->
Portfolio Manager. LangGraph, many LLM calls/ticker. Persistent memory at
~/.tradingagents/memory: realized return + alpha vs SPY reflected back into the
PM prompt each run.

How we differ (and why, deliberately):
- They use LLM agents per analyst; we use NUMERIC SignalProviders + ONE Claude
  call. Far cheaper in tokens (Todo item 2 warns about exactly this).
- They DEBATE risk with LLM personas; we use a DETERMINISTIC risk core the LLM
  cannot override. For real money this is a safety SUPERIORITY, not a gap — keep.
- Genuine gaps we adopted from them:
  [DONE] Technical analyst -> signals/technical.py (RSI/MACD/trend, yfinance, no
         key). Registered in signals/aggregator.py; SignalKind.TECHNICAL added;
         unit-tested in tests/test_technical.py (indicator math).
  [DONE] Bull/Bear debate value imported CHEAPLY: prompt now has Claude reason
         through the strongest bull AND bear case internally before setting
         conviction (no extra agents/calls). See decision/prompts.py.
  [DONE] Reflection/memory loop (their best idea; == Todo 3.1 signal attribution):
         attribution.py reconstructs closed round-trips from the ledger, scores
         each entry signal source by realized win-rate + avg P&L, and injects a
         terse "## Track record" block into the decision prompt (trusted, outside
         <market_data>). Ledger now stores entry_signals on buys + realized P&L on
         exits; the WATCHDOG also records its stops/takes/trails/flattens so
         attribution sees every close it controls. Known blind spot: exchange-side
         bracket auto-fills (no code observes them). Tests: tests/test_attribution.py.

Explicitly NOT adopting: LangGraph multi-call orchestration, LLM-debated risk
(would weaken the deterministic safety core), per-ticker multi-agent fan-out
(token cost).

--- E.11 Account-status / P&L tracker — DONE 2026-06-29 ---
status.py: the honest "are we up or down?" view, sourced from the Alpaca ACCOUNT
(the ledger can't answer it — exchange-side bracket auto-fills never pass through
our code, so ledger realized P&L structurally undercounts). compute_status():
  total_return = equity - base_value - net_cashflows   (backs out initial funding
                 AND later deposits/withdrawals; from Alpaca portfolio history)
  unrealized   = sum of open positions' unrealized P&L
  realized     = total_return - unrealized             (identity, no fill replay)
  day_pl       = equity - last_equity
New AlpacaClient.portfolio_basis() -> (base_value, net_cashflows). Verified live
(equity 100,044 / capital 100,000 -> +44 total, +6 realized; matched by hand).
`python -m investment_strategy.status` prints the report; EquityHistory persists a
once-per-day snapshot to state/equity_history.jsonl (snapshotted each decision
cycle by the orchestrator). Fails OPEN: if portfolio history is unavailable,
total/realized are None but day P&L + unrealized still render.
Tests: tests/test_status.py (identity, deposits backed out, down account, fail-open).

--- 1.1 Pattern-Day-Trader guard — DONE 2026-06-29 ---
Small MARGIN accounts (<$25k) that day-trade 4+ times in 5 business days get
flagged PDT and restricted to closing-only — and an incidental same-day watchdog
stop counts as a day trade. AccountSnapshot now carries pattern_day_trader +
daytrade_count (from Alpaca get_account). risk._pdt_block() blocks NEW opening buys
when a sub-$25k account is already PDT-flagged OR at the day-trade line
(MAX_DAY_TRADES_UNDER_25K, default 3); closing is always allowed. Inert for cash
accounts (pattern_day_trader=False/daytrade_count=0 -> exempt; the recommended
small-account setup) and for >=$25k. Toggle via PDT_GUARD_ENABLED. PDT status shown
in the account report under $25k. Tests: test_risk.py (7 cases: flagged, at-limit,
below-limit, >=25k, cash-account, disabled, sells-not-blocked).
NOTE (2026-06-29): Alpaca is MARGIN-ONLY (no cash account), so the small live float
is a margin account and the PDT guard is essential (not optional). The no-leverage
gross cap (below) ensures we still never use borrowed money.

--- 1.7 No-leverage gross-exposure cap — DONE 2026-06-29 ---
From the margin-account decision: Alpaca margin accounts offer ~2x buying power, so
relying on "cash runs out" to prevent leverage was implicit. Now explicit:
RiskLimits.max_gross_exposure_pct (env MAX_GROSS_EXPOSURE_PCT, default 100) caps the
TOTAL deployed across ALL positions to <= that % of equity. At 100 the bot can never
spend more than its own cash — no borrowed money, even though buying_power is 2x.
risk._evaluate_buy computes gross_held = sum(position market values) and refuses /
shrinks a buy that would breach the cap. Tests: test_risk.py (blocks-when-full,
does-not-use-margin-buying-power).

--- 1.3 Sector concentration cap — DONE 2026-06-29 ---
Discovery is the sole funnel and can surface several names from one hot sector
(all big-tech) that look diversified but are one bet. RiskLimits.max_sector_exposure_pct
(env MAX_SECTOR_EXPOSURE_PCT, default 30) caps total $ per sector. sectors.py
SectorMap (yfinance .info['sector'], per-cycle cached, FAILS OPEN -> unknown sector
skips the cap) supplies the sector; the orchestrator sums held positions per sector
and passes (sector, sector_exposure_usd) to risk.evaluate, which enforces the cap
(network-free gate). Tests: test_risk.py (blocks-when-full, partial-room,
skipped-when-unknown).
