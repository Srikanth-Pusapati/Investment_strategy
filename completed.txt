--- 1.7 No-leverage gross-exposure cap — DONE 2026-06-29 ---
From the margin-account decision: Alpaca margin accounts offer ~2x buying power, so
relying on "cash runs out" to prevent leverage was implicit. Now explicit:
RiskLimits.max_gross_exposure_pct (env MAX_GROSS_EXPOSURE_PCT, default 100) caps the

spend more than its own cash — no borrowed money, even though buying_power is 2x.
risk._evaluate_buy computes gross_held = sum(position market values) and refuses /
shrinks a buy that would breach the cap. Tests: test_risk.py (blocks-when-full,
does-not-use-margin-buying-power).

--- 1.5 Small-account sizing: per-trade $-risk cap + cost/slippage floor — DONE 2026-06-30 ---
Last Phase-1 safety item. Three additions to the deterministic risk gate:
  * MAX_TRADE_RISK_PCT (default 1.0) — the classic "risk 1% per trade" rule. Caps
    the ABSOLUTE $ lost if the stop fires: notional * stop% <= equity * pct. A wider
    stop now SHRINKS the position instead of the dollar risk, so a string of small
    losers stays survivable on the $100-$1000 live float. As a % it auto-scales
    (no paper-vs-live retune). 0 = off. Applied as step 2d in _evaluate_buy, after
    the gross cap, before the cash buffer.
  * EST_SLIPPAGE_PCT (default 0.10) + MIN_EDGE_RATIO (default 2.0) — cost/slippage
    edge floor. Round-trip friction ~= 2 * one-way slippage; a take-profit target
    that can't beat it by MIN_EDGE_RATIO is negative-expectancy on entry and is
    REJECTED ("friction eats the edge"). Size-independent gate, evaluated right
    after the liquidity guard. Inert for liquid names with normal targets (12% >>
    0.4% required); catches dust/tight-target degenerates. EST_SLIPPAGE_PCT=0 = off.
  * Fractional preference already covers "don't drop the sub-share remainder"
    (existing fractional path). Tiny-float sanity: orchestrator._warn_on_weak_safety_config
    now warns at startup if MAX_POSITION_PCT * equity < MIN_ORDER_USD (the per-name
    budget can't clear the min order, so no buy would ever size) — the MAX_OPEN_POSITIONS
    vs min-order recheck on a tiny float.
Config surfaced in config.py RiskLimits. Tests: test_risk.py (+6: risk cap limits
size / wider-stop-smaller / disabled; edge floor rejects-tight / allows-normal /
disabled-at-zero). Full suite green (45/45 risk).
