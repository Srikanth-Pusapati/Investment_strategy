# Run-5 day-2 review — logs, all-history backtest, and "what did we miss?"

**Date:** 2026-08-25 (run-5 day 2 of 10; config frozen Aug 24 → Sep 4).
**Scope:** (1) line-level sweep of logs Aug 23–25; (2) backtest of every flow across all six ledgers since Jun 26 (129 closed round trips); (3) first forward-return study of the signal stack; (4) architecture audit of the signal → decision → risk → exit pipeline; (5) a 14-agent adversarial verification pass over all of the above (two independent refuters per claim group + a completeness critic). Every number below survived that pass or is labelled with what the refuters found.
**Rule of the window:** nothing in this document changes strategy, prompts or knobs inside run-5. It documents what must change and when.

## 0. The answer to "did I miss something in the initial setup?"

Yes — but it is not a missing feed. News, VADER sentiment, options flow, insider, congress, lobbying, off-exchange, fundamentals and technicals are all wired and all reach the prompt. Three things were never specified, and no amount of inputs can compensate for them:

1. **No forecast target and no horizon.** The schema has no "what return, over what window" ([prompts.py:203-255](../../investment_strategy/decision/prompts.py)). The LLM is asked to pick from a slate, not to forecast. Nothing in the pipeline can be validated because nothing says what "right" means — and `signal_history` keeps only 14 days ([history.py:67](../../investment_strategy/signals/history.py)), so even now the signal → forward-return question cannot be answered on more than ~12 dates.
2. **No exposure measurement.** No beta, no net-exposure number exists anywhere in the code (grep confirms). The book's ex-ante SPY beta ranged 0.41 → 1.87 inside run-4 (1.32 the day before the −2.70% Aug 18) and sits ≈1.2 today at 50% invested. "Down-capture 1.5" is simply what a ~1×-beta book with a constant negative daily drag prints; it is not a hedging failure and not convexity (residuals on SPY-up and SPY-down days are symmetric, ±0.08%).
3. **The LLM holds the two authorities that lose money; the part that makes money is mechanical.** Mechanical profit exits (trail / bracket-take / option-take): **+$77,026 on 44 trips, 86% win rate**. LLM decision-sells: **−$26,144 net on 48 trips, 15% win rate**, median exit at 0.40× the planned stop. Options (an LLM-proposed sleeve with explicit gate exemptions): **−$24,849 on 6 trips**. Equity-only, the book is +$22,557 (PF 1.48); with everything the LLM controls, −$2,292 (PF 0.97).

"All-weather" needs alpha ≥ 0 first. With the current negative alpha (≈ −0.1 to −0.3%/day, sign robust across every cut, not yet statistically established), hedging the book to a lower beta **locks in the loss** (net-beta-0.6 overlay on the actual daily returns: −8.6% cum, Sharpe −3.7). Fix the drag, then manage exposure.

## 2. The LLM exit leak, adjudicated

Of the 40 losing decision sells: 26 bailed below half the planned stop (median 0.40×); 20 of 38 were higher five sessions later; **but 16–18 of them would have been stopped within five sessions anyway** (the backtest's "only 2 would have hit their stop" used a backward-looking MAE test and is refuted). Counterfactual of holding to the mechanical stack: +$16k (closed-only) to +$29k (with open marks), **≥80% of it from three trades (PLPC, NOK×2)** — the conclusion "LLM exits destroy value" stands; the magnitude is fat-tail dependent. The nine name-falling-defense cuts were right (median post-exit 5-day return −10%); thesis-decay and rotation cuts were wrong (+2.3% / +4.6% after exit).

Hold time: trips held <3 days −$45k (81), ≥3 days +$43k (48) — true, but it is an **outcome of the exit stack, r4-only, and top-5-concentrated**, not a rule to trade on. "Midday entries lose" is an options artifact (equity midday −$0.9k on 68 trips). The "$5.8k open-print slippage" is quote staleness in the ledger (IESC's decision quote was 6.5% below the prior close); **no fill prices are logged anywhere**, so execution quality is currently unauditable.

2. **Options: close the bypass.** Earnings blackout + anti-chase inside `evaluate_option`; remove "Earnings in" from the fallback trigger; delete the "sanctioned vehicle" prompt text; single-name bullish debit structures **off** for run-6 (index hedges/proxy puts only). *Validate:* ledger arithmetic (removes −$24.8k, forgoes one +$11.6k winner) + unit tests.
