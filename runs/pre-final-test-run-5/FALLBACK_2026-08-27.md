The bot bought a name that was **crashing ~10% on a Votorantim control-sale
headline**, then armed its own falling-name defense on the position **50 minutes
after opening it**. Root cause: the *Daily Movers* screener does not separate
up-movers from down-movers, so a crash is ingested as momentum and the composite
scored it #2.

from entry. **The process is the bug, not the P&L.**

drop `robinhood,robinhood_scans` from `SCREENER_SOURCES`."*
