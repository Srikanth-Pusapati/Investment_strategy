## Pass rules — contract v2 (`--contract v2`)

1. **Sample floor (reported, never pass/fail)**: N ≥ 24 closed round trips in the window. If N < 24 at Sep 11 close the window **extends to Sep 18**; it is not judged early.
2. **Expectancy — pooled**: mean realized_pl per closed trade > 0 at **95% one-sided** (t, df = N−1), judged on the **pool of same-config windows** (`--pool <prior trades.jsonl>`), and **only once pooled N ≥ 60**. Below 60 the checker prints per-window N / mean / t / bootstrap-95 CI and the verdict is **PENDING** (exit 3) — the next same-config window is pooled in. Run-5 is NOT same-config and is never pooled. Run-6 alone (≈10 sessions) is expected to end PENDING; that is the designed outcome, not a failure.
3. **Daily alpha (reported)**: OLS of daily book return on SPY daily return over the window — alpha/day, its t, realized beta. The t is printed but flagged "not interpretable" under **20 sessions**; it becomes a counted rule only when pooled windows reach 20 sessions (future contract revision, pre-registered then).
4. **Realized beta**: the OLS beta must lie within **±0.2 of the target 1.0** (`HEDGE_BETA_TARGET`), judged when the window has ≥ 5 sessions.
5. **Drawdown**: max drawdown of daily closing equity within the window stays **above −5%**.
6. **Capture**: judged when the window holds **≥ 4 SPY up-days AND ≥ 4 SPY down-days** (was 6/6): up-capture > down-capture vs SPY. Capture vs QQQ and IWM, and the beta-adjusted capture (book ÷ (β_ex-ante × index), β from the `book_beta_spy` stamped on each close row), are printed and recorded, not judged.
7. **Zero decision-sell losses below 0.5× the planned stop** (`(4e)` block: decision-sell rows with realized_pl_pct < 0 joined to their buy's `stop_loss_pct`). Item 2 (`LLM_SELL_AUTHORITY=events_only`) makes these impossible; a count > 0 means the gate that ran is not the one that was frozen → validity breach.

**Verdict**: GO only if every counted rule (2 when decided, 4, 5, 6 when qualified, 7) passes; PENDING if all counted rules pass but rule 2 is undecided; NO-GO otherwise.

## Amendment 2 — rule 4 graded on ex-ante beta below 20 sessions (recorded 2026-09-09 ~11:00 CT, before the Sep 9 close and before any verdict-bearing run)

**Disclosure, first:** this amendment is adopted **mid-window, with interim readings known** — realized OLS beta read +0.06 over 3 pairs on Sep 7, −0.31 over 4 pairs on Sep 8, and +0.13 over 5 sessions this morning. Mitigants, stated for the record: the defect and these exact remedy options were flagged in the Sep 7 fallback report (§6.1) *before* Sep 8's data existed; the replacement statistic was already a pre-registered, daily-recorded contract input (rule 6's `book_beta_spy` stamp), not one selected from candidates after the fact; and the verdict itself is taken only at window close (Sep 11, or Sep 18 on the sample-floor extension), which has not occurred. **Counterfactual recorded:** under rule 4 as originally written, the window was on track for NO-GO on a beta estimate whose r² sat between 0.01 and 0.22.

**The defect being amended:** rule 4 was pre-registered assuming realized ≈ ex-ante. At 4–8 return-pairs the OLS slope is unidentifiable — a single day dominates it, and the book profiting on SPY down-days (the exact all-weather behavior run-6's items 7–8 exist to produce, and which rules 3 and 6 reward) drives the slope *away* from the band. Sep 2/Sep 3 track SPY almost 1:1; Sep 4 (book +0.93% on SPY −0.39%) alone flattened beta to 0.06; Sep 8 (book +1.66% on SPY −0.55%) took it to −0.31. As written, the rule converts positive down-day alpha into a NO-GO — it punishes the strategy for working.

**The amendment:** while the graded sample has **fewer than 20 sessions** — the same interpretability floor rule 3 already pre-registers for its t-stat — rule 4 is graded on the **mean stamped ex-ante `book_beta_spy` across the window's `basis='close'` rows**, which must lie within **±0.2 of the 1.0 target** (tolerance unchanged). The realized OLS beta continues to be computed, printed, and recorded on every run — it is never silently dropped — and at **≥ 20 sessions** (pooled same-config windows, per rule 3's convention) grading reverts to realized OLS beta exactly as originally written.

**Also recorded, deliberately NOT changed:** the checker measures returns between *consecutive in-window closes*, so Sep 1 (the window's best relative day, book +0.09% vs SPY −0.69%) is silently absent from rules 3/4. Changing that convention mid-window would alter rules 3/4's inputs retroactively; it stays as-is for comparability and goes to the Sep 11 change-set.

**Implementation:** `scripts/eval_contract_check.py` (measurement tooling, outside the frozen `investment_strategy/` + `.env` surface; ops fixes are permitted by the Freeze validity condition and this one is logged in `Todo-4.txt`).
