WHY THIS FILE EXISTS (the 2026-07-01 diagnosis)
  Observation: a full trading day produced trades in only ONE name (SPG), and the
  account sat ~90% in CASH ($90k of $100k idle). Root-cause dig showed:
    - Discovery is HEALTHY: the screener surfaced 12 good names (AMD, TDG, HUBB,
      DASH, ...). NOT the bottleneck.
    - Bottleneck is DOWNSTREAM: Claude proposed only 2-3 buys/cycle and HELD the
      rest, each buy capped ~5% (MAX_POSITION_PCT), and it kept re-buying the
      already-capped held name. Net: capital deployed glacially.
    - You cannot beat a FULLY-invested index while 90% in cash — it's arithmetic,
      not stock-picking. Cash is a structural SHORT against the benchmark.
    - Two of three discovery feeds are DEAD: insider + options_flow returned 0;
      only congress (45-day lag, weakest signal) is producing.
