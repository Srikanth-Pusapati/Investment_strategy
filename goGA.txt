PHASE GA-0 — DEFINE "GA": LEGAL POSTURE + VENDOR/PLATFORM TERMS  (GATE;
~1-2 weeks for the (a)-track answers; the (b)-track written work is longer)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The one decision that shapes everything below. Three product shapes, in
increasing legal weight:
  (a) SELF-HOSTED — each user runs their OWN instance with their OWN keys
    (broker, Anthropic, Quiver) on their own machine/VPS. We ship software,
    not advice. Lightest regulatory surface (disclaimers + license). G.4
    secrets vault mostly unnecessary; G.1-G.3 become nice-to-have. Fastest
    path to a real GA. BYO-keys is also what keeps most vendor-ToS problems
    on the user's side of the line (see GA-0.4).
  (b) HOSTED SaaS — we run per-user instances; users connect their broker
    keys; trades execute on THEIR accounts. Custody-free but "investment
    advice delivered via software" questions; state-by-state RIA analysis;
    redistribution licensing for every data feed (GA-0.4).
  (c) MANAGED MONEY — we trade pooled/managed funds. RIA/BD registration,
    audits, compliance officer. OUT OF SCOPE for GA-1 (Todo-3 L.1 hard gate).
